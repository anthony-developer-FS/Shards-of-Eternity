How to play

  • The timeline shows who acts next. Faster units act more often.
  • On a hero's turn: move once and act once, in either order.
  • Tap a highlighted tile to move. Tap a ringed enemy to preview, tap again to attack. With a mouse, hover to preview and click once.
  • Enemies sleep until a hero comes within 5 tiles in sight.
  • Nobody can move through a hero or end on an occupied tile. Enemies can pass through each other.
  • Leaving an enemy's side gives it a free attack.
  • Rift pressure rises each round once the room wakes. At 4 reinforcements arrive. At 6 the walls shift, and glowing tiles show where one round ahead.
  • Keys: A ability, E end turn, Esc cancel.
